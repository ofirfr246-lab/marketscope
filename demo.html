Executive summary

For “magnetic phone mount” in the United States, MarketScope measures about 8,100 monthly searches, with a roughly stable 12-month trend. Demand scores 76/100, competition 90/100 and saturation 64/100, producing an Opportunity score of 48/100 and a verdict of UNDERPERFORM — high competition versus solid demand. We counted 65 Google Shopping listings from 29 sellers, priced from $6.68 to $69. A reasonable selling band is $14.99–$19.99, at or just below the median of $19.99. The leading visible sellers by listing share are Home Depot (12%), Target (11%), Best Buy (11%), Walmart (8%) and Five Below (5%). Amazon, Meta Ad Library, TikTok and AliExpress are not in this report — those sources are coming soon.
