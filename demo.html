The US wireless earbuds market shows strong and rising demand, but competition is concentrated among a few established stores. The clearest opening is the $30–40 price band: it holds only 9% of listings while sitting between the two largest clusters of demand. A mid-priced product with strong reviews and clear differentiation (battery life, fit, or call quality) has room to compete. Expect high ad costs in Q4.
