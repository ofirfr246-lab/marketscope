FAQ

Questions

Does Josy post to my social accounts?

No. Josy writes and organises the content; you copy and publish it. She has no access to your social accounts or analytics.

Where do the numbers come from?

Only from the live demand tool: search data that MarketScope fetches from DataForSEO at the moment you ask. Each reply with numbers shows a “Search data” source chip and the time it was fetched.

Which languages does Josy speak?

Josy answers in the language you write in, including Hebrew and English. For Israel, demand checks can use Hebrew keywords.

What happens when I run out of messages?

Your daily allowance resets at 00:00 UTC. Upgrading your MarketScope plan raises the limit.

Can Josy run paid ads?

Not yet. In the campaign manager you can build complete paid campaign drafts (campaign, ad sets / ad groups, ads) and plan organic posts. Publishing to ad accounts is coming soon and will need each platform's approval.
