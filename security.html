// TRUST CENTER

Security and privacy, built in from day one.

How we protect your data, what we collect, and where we're headed on compliance. We describe our practices and goals honestly; we don't claim certifications we don't hold.

Early access: MarketScope is pre-launch. The practices below describe how the platform is being designed and the standards we commit to at launch.
◆ Encrypted in transit (TLS)◆ Encryption at rest◆ Stripe-ready payments◆ GDPR-minded◆ Least-privilege access
ENC

Encryption

All traffic is served over HTTPS/TLS. Customer data is designed to be stored with encryption at rest using our cloud provider's managed encryption.

PAY

Payments

Payments are designed to run through Stripe, a PCI DSS–compliant processor. MarketScope never stores full card numbers.

IAM

Access control

Our standard: least-privilege access for our team, multi-factor authentication on internal systems, and audit logging of administrative actions.

INF

Infrastructure

Built to run on established cloud infrastructure with isolated environments, automated backups and dependency monitoring.

DAT

Data minimization

We collect what we need to run the service: account details, billing records and the research you run. We don't sell personal data.

AI

AI & your data

Your research is used to generate your reports. We don't use your private research to train public models.
