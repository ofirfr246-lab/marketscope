MarketScope vs typical product research tools.

Product-spy and ad-library tools are great for inspiration. MarketScope is built for a different job: deciding whether, and where, to compete.

Typical product research tools MarketScope
Starting question"What's trending right now?""Can my product win in this market?"
InputBrowse a feed of productsYou choose the product and the market
Primary dataAds, creatives and best-seller listsLive Google Shopping and Search, with Amazon, ads and supplier cost coming soon on higher plans
OutputProduct and store listsFour scores, a verdict and an AI explanation
Explains its reasoningRarelyEvery score links to its data
Over timeSnapshotsMarket Terminal trends and Market Watch alerts
Pricing modelFlat subscriptionPlan quotas + pay-per-use credits
