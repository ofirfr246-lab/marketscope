BILLING FAQ

Pricing questions

Is the Free plan really free?

Yes. You get 5 credits with live Google Shopping and Google Search data, no credit card required. Amazon is included in the Free plan but is coming soon. Upgrade whenever you need more.

How does yearly billing work?

Pay for 10 months and get 12: yearly billing gives you 2 months free. You still receive your monthly credit quota every month.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Yes, anytime from your account. Upgrades apply immediately; downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of your current billing period.

Do credits expire?

Monthly plan credits refresh each cycle and don't roll over. Top-up credits remain in your Credit Wallet while your account is active.

Which data sources does each plan use?

Live today: Google Shopping and Google Search, on every plan — enough to start answering “Is there demand?”. Amazon is in Free and Starter but coming soon. Plus will add Meta Ad Library and TikTok ads and Creative Center trends, and Pro will add AliExpress supplier cost, order volume and a true margin calculator, plus competitor Shopify stores — store count, price bands, top stores and newest launches. Those sources keep their plan mapping and are labelled Coming soon until they go live.

How do I pay?

Payments are processed on Stripe's secure Payment Link page. You'll be signed in first, then sent to Stripe with your account already attached. MarketScope never stores your full card number. After payment, Stripe tells us to apply your plan or credit pack. You can manage or cancel a subscription from the dashboard via Stripe's customer portal.

Do you offer refunds?

See our Refund & Credits Policy. In short: if something goes wrong on our side, we make it right.

Do you have team or agency plans?

Pro includes 3 team seats and white-label PDF reports (early access). Larger agency workspaces are on the roadmap. Contact us to join the early group.
