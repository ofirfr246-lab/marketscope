FAQ

Questions, answered.

What exactly does MarketScope analyze?

You pick a product and a target market. MarketScope gathers Google Shopping results, competitor stores and prices, search demand and market presence, then turns them into Demand, Competition, Saturation and Opportunity scores with a final verdict.

Does a STRONG BUY mean my product will succeed?

No. We don't sell promises. A verdict summarizes what the market data says today. Execution, branding, margins and marketing are still yours. We help you make that decision with far better information.

How is this different from product research or ad-spy tools?

Those tools help you find trending products or copy ads. MarketScope starts from the product you already have in mind and tells you whether, and where, it's worth competing.

What are credits?

Credits are how you pay for research. Each plan includes a monthly credit quota, and the Credit Wallet lets you top up with pay-per-use credits whenever you need more.

Which data sources are included?

Live today: Google Shopping and Google Search, on every plan. Amazon is included in Free and Starter but is coming soon. Plus will add Meta Ad Library and TikTok ads and Creative Center trends, and Pro will add AliExpress supplier cost, order volume and a true margin calculator, plus competitor Shopify stores — store count, price bands, top stores and newest launches. Those sources are also coming soon.

Which markets are supported?

We're launching with major e-commerce markets such as the US, UK and EU countries, and expanding from there. Tell us which market you need when you join the waitlist.

When does MarketScope launch?

We're onboarding early users in waves. Join the waitlist and you'll be among the first to get access, with free credits to start.
