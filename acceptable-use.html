Acceptable Use Policy

Rules that keep MarketScope fair, safe and reliable for everyone.

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Early-access version

You may not

  • Resell, redistribute or publish raw MarketScope data at scale without permission
  • Scrape, crawl or bulk-extract the service, or bypass rate limits or credit metering
  • Share account credentials or use one account for multiple organizations to avoid plan limits
  • Reverse engineer the scoring models or software
  • Use MarketScope to research illegal, counterfeit, or prohibited products
  • Upload malware, attack, probe or overload our systems
  • Use outputs to harass, defame or infringe the rights of others

Store Intelligence

You may use Store Intelligence only to research publicly available competitor storefronts and public ad libraries, for lawful competitive analysis. You may not use the product (or any data we later collect) to:

  • Bypass logins, paywalls, bot protections, CAPTCHAs or other access controls
  • Collect personal data about a store’s customers, employees or identifiable visitors
  • Access private dashboards, admin areas, or non-public APIs
  • Harass a competitor, scrape a store in a way that disrupts it, or violate that store’s terms

Early-access profiles shown today are labelled examples, not live crawls.

Fair use

Plans include generous limits for normal business use. We may contact you or apply limits if usage threatens the stability of the service.

Enforcement

We may warn, suspend or terminate accounts that violate this policy. Serious violations may be reported to authorities.

Reporting

Report abuse through our contact form.
