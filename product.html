Paste & Analyze EARLY ACCESS

Paste a product link from Amazon, AliExpress, a Shopify store, TikTok Shop, eBay, Etsy or Walmart. MarketScope detects the source from the URL and starts a report in one click.

  • Source auto-detected from the URL pattern (logo and name chip)
  • Product name and market are guessed from the link where possible
  • Same scores, verdict and AI Advisor as a typed report
  • A Chrome extension is planned so you can run this from the product page itself
Early access: detection is client-side from the URL only. We do not fetch or scrape the page yet, so price and category stay empty until live extraction is connected. Once a report runs, scores come from live Google Shopping and Google Search data. Amazon, Meta, TikTok and AliExpress are coming soon.

AI Advisor

An analyst that reads the data with you. It explains what the scores mean, flags risks and gaps, and answers follow-up questions, always citing the numbers behind its reasoning.

  • Risks, opportunities and gaps in plain language
  • Every claim linked to the data that supports it
  • Follow-up questions on your report
  • Never a promise. Just analysis.

Market Terminal

The Bloomberg-style view. Charts and trends over time for prices, demand, competition and your opportunity score, so you see where a market is going, not only where it is.

  • Line and candlestick charts with 1D / 1W / 1M / 6M / 1Y toggles
  • Ticker tape, movers, news-style desk feed
  • Category indices, heatmap, screener and side-by-side compare
  • Watchlist alerts, portfolio, 6-month backtest, events and niche ‘earnings’
  • Store Intelligence: competitor storefront profiles (early access, example data)
  • History depth grows with your plan — all current figures are labelled examples

STO

Store Intelligence EARLY ACCESS

Enter a competitor store URL. MarketScope detects the platform from the link and opens a store profile: catalog size, bestseller-style products, price distribution, a new-product and price-change timeline, a modelled traffic band, public-ad-library creatives, and review sentiment.

  • Shopify, WooCommerce and similar public storefronts
  • Track store — alerts for a new product, a price change, or a new public ad
  • Uses only publicly available information; no personal data; no login bypass
Early access: live collection is not connected. Directory profiles and any URL you paste are labelled example or placeholder output.
