// ABOUT

We think founders deserve the same quality of information as traders.

MarketScope started with a simple frustration: e-commerce founders make big bets on products with almost no structured data about the market they're entering.

Why we're building this

Most tools in this space are built to help you find a "winning product": a trending item, a viral ad, a store to copy. They're useful for inspiration, but they don't answer the question that actually costs money: should I enter this market, with this product, right now?

Financial markets solved this decades ago. Traders don't buy on a hunch; they look at a terminal full of data, trends and analysis. We're building that terminal for e-commerce.

What we believe

  • Honesty over hype. We don't sell a promise that a product will succeed. We sell better information, structured analysis, and smarter decisions.
  • Transparency. Every score and verdict should be explainable, down to the data points behind it.
  • Your idea first. We test the product you chose instead of pushing you toward what everyone else is selling.
  • Fair pricing. Pay for the research you run, with clear credit costs before every analysis.

Who we are

MarketScope is an independent, founder-led company from Israel, building for e-commerce entrepreneurs worldwide. We're early, we're shipping fast, and we'd love to hear from you.
