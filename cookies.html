How MarketScope uses cookies and similar technologies like local storage.

What we use

We use essential cookies and browser local storage to run the site and remember your choices (for example, your cookie preference, waitlist confirmation, and — once accounts are live — your sign-in session). Optional analytics cookies are used only if you choose "Accept all".

Categories

  • Essential: required for the site to function and for security. Always on.
  • Preferences: remember settings such as your cookie choice.
  • Analytics (optional): help us understand how the site is used. Off unless you allow them.

Third parties

This site loads web fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of delivering the font files. Once accounts are enabled, Supabase acts as our authentication and database processor and stores the session in your browser so you stay signed in. If you use “Continue with Google”, Google’s sign-in flow applies its own cookie policy. Payment pages will be served by our payment processor under their own cookie policy.

Your choices

You can change your preference at any time using "Cookie settings" in the footer, or clear cookies and local storage in your browser settings.
